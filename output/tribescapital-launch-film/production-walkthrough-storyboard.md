# TribesCapital Production Walkthrough

## Capture Scope

- Production origin: `https://community.tribes.capital/`.
- Captured on 2026-10-09 at a 1920 x 1080 browser viewport. Internal views keep the same browser URL; the labels below identify the observed in-app view, not invented URL paths.
- Signed-in coverage is the authorized `Community Member` account. The account identity, contact address, member-generated forum content, message previews, and notification contents are masked or omitted from the rendered capture assets.
- No account, project, course, or event was created; no question or message was sent; no RSVP remains active. Password fields are always empty in captured frames.
- All scenes use real production screenshots. The cursor/highlight is a motion-graphics overlay; it does not imply an action succeeded unless the before/after state was actually observed.

## Access and Verification Limits

- Verified in production: public sign-in, account creation form, administrator sign-in form, password-reset request form, and the signed-in Community Member views listed above.
- Not accessed: administrator console, Investor-only Due Diligence Vault, Contractor directory, Facility Operator experience, Investor experience, Read-only Guest experience, OAuth provider completion, password-reset completion, course lesson/player, and any route not exposed by the signed-in Community Member navigation.
- The platform tour and Help categories describe Contractors and the Due Diligence Vault, but neither appears in this account's navigation; their live pages are not claimed as inspected.
- Messaging showed a live inbox but also intermittent Socket.IO WebSocket handshake/close errors. Conversation contents and realtime delivery are not verified.
- Production event data included a past-dated item in the Upcoming list and a calendar month that did not match the capture date. The film should not characterize the displayed list as a fully validated schedule.
- An RSVP was activated accidentally while locating an unlabeled event-panel close icon, then canceled immediately. The final account state showed zero RSVPs; no RSVP remains active.

## Render and Review

```sh
python3 make_walkthrough_soundtrack.py soundtrack-walkthrough.wav
swift walkthrough.swift --output-dir . --audio soundtrack-walkthrough.wav
```
